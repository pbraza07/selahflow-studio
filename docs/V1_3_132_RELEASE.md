# v1.3.132 — client contacts and business-branded communication emails

Owners open **Settings → Client communications**. Contact sync reads this business’s current Clients directory sources: managed profiles, appointment history, pending/declined bookings and memberships. Clients without valid email addresses are counted but not selectable. Archived/deleted clients are excluded, duplicate family addresses combine into one recipient, and unsubscribe preferences survive subsequent syncs. Edit contacts in Clients and sync again; there is no duplicate editable contact store or external device/Google address-book OAuth integration.

1. Click Sync client contacts, search and select individual or matching contacts (up to 500 unique email addresses per campaign).
2. Enter subject and message; optionally use `{{client_name}}` / `{{business_name}}` and a labeled HTTPS action button. Save business branding/logo changes through the existing controls first.
3. Preview the exact business logo/theme layout and recipient list. Preview is private, sandboxed and sends nothing. Changes to the form clear the preview; stale recipient/branding state or previews older than 24h require a fresh preview.
4. Click Send and confirm the count/subject. Each recipient receives a separate email; addresses are not exposed through CC/BCC. Communications save independently of the business settings form. The configured email provider must be connected.

Campaign history refreshes while the tab is open and tracks submitted, pending/retrying, failed/unconfigured, unknown and skipped counts. Submitted means provider acceptance, not inbox delivery. Cancel unsent stops remaining messages; already-processing messages may arrive. The existing in-app worker processes up to five campaign recipients per cycle after transactional work, with durable claims and provider idempotency keys. Failed/unconfigured sends use at most three attempts with backoff. Ambiguous sends become unknown and are not blindly replayed. Background progress requires a running app instance and resumes on startup.

Emails use the current saved business branding through the existing Gmail/Resend delivery layer; the shared sender remains configured as before. Missing logos use business-name fallback. Body and headers are escaped/sanitized; custom buttons require HTTPS. Each email includes a private, business-specific unsubscribe link. GET displays confirmation without changing state, protecting against link scanners; same-origin POST suppresses future general communication emails only, including queued messages not already sending. Booking, approval, payment, membership and other transactional messages retain their existing behavior. Staging recipient restrictions remain enforced.

Migration 057 creates three business-scoped tables and indexes with a composite campaign/business foreign key. No existing business preference, Stripe/provider configuration or existing financial record is modified. Server-side ownership/origin checks protect all owner operations. Contacts and opt-outs are rechecked before each send; changed or deleted identities are skipped. Permanent client deletion removes related structured recipient PII and protects in-flight sends while retaining campaign content/history and unrelated clients/required financials.

Verification pending. Automated tests use synthetic PostgreSQL tenants, component handlers and mocked provider delivery. No real client campaigns, bookings, charges, emails, contact preference mutations or deletions are used for QA. Actual signed-in owner/mobile browser acceptance and real inbox delivery are not manually verified.
