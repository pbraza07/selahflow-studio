# SelahFlow waiver amendments — v1.3.234

These are the two new sections at the top of the revised waiver. The app inserts each business’s name. Existing sections follow with updated numbering.

## 1. Recurring membership payments and cancellation

If I enroll in a recurring membership and separately authorize recurring billing, my membership renews automatically and the authorized payment method is charged the amount and at the frequency disclosed at enrollment until I cancel. I may cancel using the cancellation instructions provided at enrollment or by contacting [Business name] through its published contact details. I must cancel before the next scheduled renewal to stop that renewal, subject to the plan terms and applicable law. Not attending sessions does not cancel the membership. One-time session packages do not renew automatically. Signing this waiver alone does not enroll me in a membership or authorize a payment.

## 2. 24-hour notice and missed-session deduction

If I cannot attend a booked session, I must notify [Business name] through its published contact details at least 24 hours before the scheduled start time. If I miss the session without providing that notice, one session will be deducted from the applicable membership or session-package balance. The deduction applies once to that missed appointment. A cancellation reported at least 24 hours in advance does not incur this no-show deduction. Any exception approved by the business or required by applicable law will be honored. Canceling an individual session does not cancel a recurring membership.

Activation: the platform administrator must record the completed legal-review reference and date for the revised document, then save the business settings. Existing reviewed wording remains active until then. Clients sign the revised waiver before their next waiver-required booking after activation; historical signed documents are retained.
