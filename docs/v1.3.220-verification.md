## Changes

- The owner bell and device-notification control share a toolbar group. The mobile bell is below the open menu/backdrop, and inactive workspace content has an isolated stacking context.
- The booking form offers explicit device opt-in before submission. The successful booking registers the chosen subscription with its private token before checkout can redirect. Denial or subscription failure preserves the booking and its retry controls. Existing approval and payment eligibility remain authoritative.
- Automated client email HTML and plain text include the current business's exact Google review link. Gmail and Resend share the same template. The review action is separate from payment actions; owner/staff alerts omit it. Missing or invalid Google listing identities produce no guessed review destination. Owners manage the listing in Website & branding.
