# SelahFlow v1.3.221 verification

## Changes

Daily pending-payment reminders run after 09:00 America/New_York, at least 24 hours after the payment request or known due event. A business/client receives one consolidated email per Eastern calendar day, containing eligible unpaid sessions, initial membership/package balances and actual outstanding recurring Stripe invoices. Existing and future businesses default to enabled; owners can pause under Settings → Notifications.

Eligibility is refreshed immediately before delivery. Paid, free, membership-covered, cancelled, declined, no-show and removed-service session quotes are excluded. Existing provider payment references are checked read-only to suppress paid or processing obligations despite delayed local reconciliation. Recurring balances use current open Stripe invoices, never estimated subscription debt. Existing discounted checkout URLs retain their original terms; expired checkouts without a safe reconstruction use contact-business guidance.

Atomic database claims and per-business/recipient/day uniqueness prevent concurrent duplicate dispatch. Known delivery failures can retry; uncertain outcomes are not replayed that day. Old queued reminders expire rather than being delivered as a backlog. Queues store source identities rather than email bodies or payment links. Private automated membership links are separate from manual invitation counters and bound to the current recipient. Additive migrations 092/093 retain historical data.

## Validation

Focused PGlite tests exercise real migrations, concurrent workers, next-day recurrence, payment/email/amount changes before send, claim loss, ambiguous delivery, provider reconnection, private links, membership eligibility, current invoice amounts and ownership, coupon preservation and delayed payment reconciliation. Existing payment and notification regression suites remain required.

The rendered branded email was inspected in a browser at 375-pixel phone and 760-pixel desktop widths with synthetic session, membership invoice and onsite package examples. Labels, amounts, buttons and explanatory text wrap legibly. No real client message or payment was used for QA. Staging reminders use allowlist-only delivery protection, including for copied historical client records.

Final full-suite, TypeScript, production build, CI and exact LIVE deployment results are pending. Real-provider inbox receipt is not tested; acceptance by an email provider is not proof of inbox delivery. Off-app payments must be recorded in SelahFlow to stop their reminders.
