# v1.3.130 — business email branding

Business-related emails sent by SelahFlow now use the originating business’s current saved **Branding** theme and uploaded logo. Change colors through the existing owner branding controls and upload/change/remove the logo there; no separate email settings are required. The sender name/address and connected Gmail/Resend account remain configured as before. The header, page/card/footer, accents and action buttons use theme colors, with readable text colors derived from their backgrounds. A small footer identifies delivery via SelahFlow.

Coverage: client booking submissions, approval/payment invitations, booking payment confirmations, membership payment invitations/receipts, booking-review and membership owner alerts, and registration verification codes. Workers pass canonical business IDs from their database records; registration verification uses the resolved owner identity. Branding loads at delivery, so future sends and unsent queued messages pick up saved changes. Already-delivered messages remain unchanged. No queue replay or mass resend is introduced. Platform-only messages with no business context retain SelahFlow branding.

Logos are read from the existing business_logos table for the same active business, normalized from supported PNG/JPG/WebP into a bounded inline PNG and embedded via matching Content-ID attachments in Gmail and Resend. No external logo URL or cross-business cache is used. Missing or damaged logos fall back to that business’s name. Missing/unavailable business context fails the scoped send rather than substituting another business. Theme colors and names are escaped/validated before rendering; secure payment/review links, plain text, sender authorization, staging delivery restrictions and idempotency remain intact.

No database migration, branding preference mutation, provider credential change, real customer test email, payment or deletion. Sharp 0.35.5 is now a pinned direct dependency; the same version was already installed by Next. Automated results and staging/production deployment evidence pending; inbox/client rendering acceptance has not been performed.
