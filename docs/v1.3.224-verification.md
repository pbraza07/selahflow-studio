## Behavior

The owner types DELETE after a prompt explaining immediate linked-subscription cancellation and enrollment removal. The server requires this confirmation and uses the authenticated business context. A completed free checkout can supply a subscription ID that was not saved locally after an earlier activation failure. The connected account, checkout reference, metadata, customer and subscription are verified before cancellation. Cancellation sets invoice_now=false and prorate=false. Open unpaid checkouts are expired; settled financial facts and bookings are retained. Positive or processing pending payments still require reconciliation. No schema changes.
