# v1.3.129 — bookable session removal

In **Settings → Bookable sessions**, select **Remove session**, confirm the removal, then **Save business settings**. Removing a recurring definition removes its published recurring and rescheduled dates. The screenshot error occurred because the series was removed while its saved reschedule remained; both client and server now clean linked overrides. Session-specific question targets are cleaned too: questions for remaining sessions stay scoped; a question losing its last session keeps membership use if applicable or is removed from active question configuration, with saved historical answers untouched. It is never silently broadened to all booking services.

Existing appointments, pending requests, financial records, signed waivers, attendance and private tracking records are retained. Removal stops publishing the session; it does not cancel booked customers, refund payments or delete their records. Retained appointments appear individually on the owner calendar and continue blocking their booked time. Pending requests remain in owner review; new attempts to book the removed session are rejected. Unrelated service booking availability continues under existing business rules. Use normal booking management to cancel/reschedule retained bookings as needed. Retained session definitions still cannot have their booked schedule/duration/capacity changed unsafely; use Reschedule · keep customers.

Shared owner-scoped Settings implementation; no schema/provider/environment changes or real customer QA mutations. Automated checks, staging/production deployment evidence and owner browser testing limitations pending.
