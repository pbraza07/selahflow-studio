# v1.3.236 — Product catalog and checkout

Status: v1.3.236 verified LIVE in staging and production on November 12, 2026. Later releases have since advanced production to v1.3.241; this document records the product release evidence.

## Requested behavior

- Owners add/edit/archive/restore products under Service catalog, with price, description and optional photo. Images are decoded, auto-oriented, stripped of metadata, resized to at most 1,000 pixels and compressed to WebP at most 250 KB. Input limit: 5 MB and 25 million pixels.
- Services and active products share the package service allowance. Both mutation paths take the same transaction lock and honor individual business overrides. Archived product/order snapshots remain retained.
- Business public pages display a horizontally scrollable carousel with previous/next controls. `/shop/{slug}` offers product quantities, client identification, a tax review and Stripe or on-site cash/Zelle checkout.
- The user explicitly confirmed **first purchase per client**, not per product or once per business. Exactly 10% of the first paid product order subtotal is charged, excluding tax, with no $5 minimum. Subsequent product orders do not incur another fee. Existing booking and membership policies remain independent.
- Client first/last name plus one of email, phone or Member / Client ID is required. Identity matching is business-scoped; contact collisions require disambiguation. The public API does not reveal saved client contacts. Paid purchases record product names, quantities, tax and payment method in the existing financial event/client-history pipeline.
- Cash and Zelle remain pending until an authenticated owner confirms actual receipt. Existing monthly commission statements collect the separate product fee only after product authorization and verified cash-billing authorization.
- Owners explicitly confirm pickup address, business tax liability and product fee terms. The separate authorization supplements the older cash terms’ retail exclusion; its text and actor are retained. No business consent is auto-accepted.
- Stripe Tax must be active with an active registration matching the pickup jurisdiction. Each owner selects the applicable product tax code. Product pickup locations scope tax at the business; customer billing address is collected for tax treatment. Stripe calculates rates, not a hardcoded percentage. Native Checkout handles online tax transactions and tax refund reversals; cash/Zelle tax transactions use the standalone Tax API after receipt confirmation.
- Product checkout opens only after business setup. Creating products does not require connected Stripe. No real payment or tax registration is created by deployment.

## Owner workflow

1. Open Business settings → Service catalog → Products for sale. Add a product name, price, applicable Stripe tax category and optional picture. Products save immediately and use one catalog slot each; archive a product to free its active slot without removing sale history.
2. In Product checkout & local tax setup, enter the pickup address after completing the business's Stripe Tax settings/registrations. Review the product fee terms, confirm tax responsibility and choose Verify & save product checkout. For cash/Zelle, complete monthly cash-commission card authorization in Payment setup as well.
3. Share View product shop. Clients select quantities, enter their name and phone/email/Member ID, provide their billing address, review calculated tax and choose Stripe, cash or Zelle. Product sales are pickup-only.
4. Confirm an on-site product receipt under Product orders only after the full cash or Zelle amount is actually received. Stripe receipts are confirmed from verified provider notifications. Product names and payment facts appear in client history and financial reports.

## Operational qualifications

Owners must configure and verify their own Stripe merchant/Tax registrations, pickup address, product tax categories and product authorization. On-site product checkout also requires verified monthly commission billing. Adding a tax registration in Stripe is distinct from registering with a tax authority; no registrations are created here. No shipping flow or stock-count inventory was requested. Actual signed-in owner/mobile acceptance and real Stripe tax calculations/payments must be recorded separately if performed.

Explicit permanent client deletion expires pending product Stripe checkouts first, cancels unpaid on-site orders and anonymizes retained product accounting references. Original financial facts and commission eligibility records remain retained. Production Connect webhook already includes asynchronous success notifications.
