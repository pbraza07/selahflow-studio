## Setup in SelahFlow

1. Primary administrator: sign into SelahFlow with the registered primary administrator account. Open Platform Admin → First-time customer commissions. The current browser's business-owner account cannot perform this step.
2. Confirm Stripe commission setup reports Ready. When missing events are listed, Configure commission webhook events adds them only to the existing exact matching endpoint; it does not replace the endpoint URL, mode, signing secret or other destinations. If no unambiguous enabled matching endpoint exists, the administrator must review the existing Stripe Connect webhook. The application intentionally does not create a new secret or overwrite server credentials.
3. Each business owner: open Settings → Stripe payments and complete/verify that business's Stripe connection.
4. In First-time customer commissions, choose Enable 10% on first paid bookings and Enable 10% on first paid memberships. Review the fee terms, accept them as an authorized representative, then select Save commission settings. The business name/workspace determines the fee scope. Saving the general business settings does not activate these separate controls.
5. New eligible checkouts apply the fee automatically. Stripe sends the application fee to SelahFlow's platform balance; the connected business receives its proceeds less the fee and applicable processing costs. Bank payouts follow the Stripe account's payout configuration. Existing opened checkouts retain their quoted fee; expire an unpaid checkout before replacing it.
6. Commission activity records paid/refunded fees independently from gross customer revenue. Repeat bookings and membership renewals have no new commission. A refund does not reset first-purchase eligibility. For a $100 first booking, the platform fee is $10; for a later $200 first membership, its separate platform fee is $20; later renewals have $0 additional commission. The client price is unchanged.

The app calculates fees; there is no separate global Stripe Dashboard 10% rule to enable. Eligibility is independent per category and business, using the verified client identity rather than a shared family email or reusable Unique ID alone. Known prior cash/Stripe payment and membership histories protect existing purchases. Booking clients already present at activation and imported clients are exempt from the new booking fee. Missing legacy identity information is not guessed from email. Paid or processing checkouts cannot be expired. If initial membership provisioning is delayed beyond its paid period, the app requests business reconciliation instead of creating a surprise catch-up charge.
